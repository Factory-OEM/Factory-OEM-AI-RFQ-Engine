📖 What is this?
Factory-OEM AI RFQ Engine is a free, AI-powered cost-estimation tool for global apparel buyers, sourcing managers, and independent brands.

It removes the guesswork from overseas manufacturing. Instead of sending spec sheets back and forth for days, you simply upload a product image and receive a bottom-up cost breakdown from four of China's most competitive apparel manufacturing clusters — complete with sampling fees and tiered bulk pricing.
